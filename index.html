Zombie Outbreak

You are patient zero, alone in the city. Infect citizens to grow your horde — the glowing one is you. Clear all 3 levels; each one is a fresh city and a bigger crowd.

Drag the joystick to move (or use arrow keys / WASD). Get close to a human to infect them — they'll join your horde and help hunt the rest.
