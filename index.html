Zombie Outbreak

You are patient zero, alone in the city. Infect citizens to grow your horde — the glowing blue one is you. Clear all 3 levels; each one is a fresh city and a bigger crowd. Once you infect someone, a second joystick appears so you can point your horde in a direction. Use your sprint when it's charged to close a gap.

Drag the joystick to move (or use arrow keys / WASD). Get close to a human to infect them — they'll join your horde. Once you have a pal, a second joystick points your horde in a direction; let go and they hunt on their own again. Tap the bolt to sprint once it's charged.
